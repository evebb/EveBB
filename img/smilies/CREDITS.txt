licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

  https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji
  https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Files are mapped to eveBB's smiley codes (see include/parser.php). Replacing a
file with another PNG of the same name changes that smiley everywhere - in
rendered posts and in the visual editor's emoticon picker.
